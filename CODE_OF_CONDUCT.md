# Code of conduct

We want OpenMirror to be a friendly place to build things, whoever you are and whatever you are building with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with beginners; everyone started somewhere.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or other people's private information.
- Do not publish keys, passwords or private data in issues, pull requests or logs. Remove them first.

## What happens when someone does not

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who repeatedly or seriously break these rules. If you are treated badly, or see it happen, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's *Security* tab or the maintainers' profile pages; reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
